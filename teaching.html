Teaching

Classrooms are civic spaces, too.

Four courses at the Kennedy School and an executive education program for city leaders, for students who want to study democracy and practice it.

Teaching philosophy

What we try to do in a classroom.

Teaching democracy at a policy school is a specific responsibility. Many students will soon be making decisions that shape whether other people have real voice in campaigns, in agencies, in philanthropy, in movements.

The courses pair close reading of theory and empirical scholarship with experiential learning: case-based analysis, fieldwork, simulations, and strategic projects for real campaigns. Practitioners often guest lecture.

Courses
Academic year 2025–26
API-203Harvard Kennedy School

Qualitative Methods

Spring · MPP Core · ~90 students

The HKS qualitative methods core. Interviews, ethnography/fieldwork, focus groups, coding, and the design of small-N studies for policy and political analysis. Example teaching artifact: HKS focus-group site on student AI use.

MLD-370Harvard Kennedy School

Social Movements: The Art and Science of Social Change

Fall · MPP / MC-MPA

A course on social movements as both subject and practice. Students read the empirical and theoretical literature on strategy, mobilization, and organization, and design a campaign for a real movement.

MLD-615Harvard Kennedy School

Mixed Methods Analytics for Leaders & Policymakers

Spring · MPP / MC-MPA

A practicum in research design for people who run organizations. Students bring a real question and leave with a mixed-methods plan that combines administrative data, field observation, surveys, and small experiments.

NEW · 2026Harvard Kennedy School

AI and Civil Society: Human Agency in the Age of Artificial Agents

Fall 2026

What happens to civic life when machines start trying to do the things human organizers do: recruiting supporters, asking for time, building relationships? A new course on agency, automation, and the democratic institutions that (still) depend on human judgment.

Executive education

Leading Civic Engagement in Cities.

Bloomberg Harvard · Biennial

A weeklong program for senior city staff working on civic engagement. Co-chaired with Archon Fung, convened biennially at the Harvard Kennedy School. Faculty and practitioners across organizing, public administration, and digital democracy.
