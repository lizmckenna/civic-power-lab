Practice

Research is one half. Building is the other.

Practice at the lab means the tools, trainings, and field collaborations we ship alongside the research. The R&D arm of democracy needs an arm, this is it.

Why a practice tab

The field already learns. We're trying to help it learn faster.

The dominant civic-tech platforms are investor-owned, expensive, and designed to retain users rather than develop their practice. When organizations can't systematically capture or learn from their relational data, field-wide learning is impossible.

We want to change that, with open-source infrastructure, shared measurement, training that meets organizers where they are, and collaboration with the funders and intermediaries already working on the same problem.

Tool 01 · Headliner
Alpha in 2026

Groundwork.

An open-source relational organizing platform, built by and for the field. Structured records of recruitment channels, one-on-ones, commitment follow-through, leadership development, team formation, and event participation, all linked to individual contacts and organizational outcomes over time.

Why now

AI-assisted tooling now makes it feasible to build production-quality alternatives to incumbent civic tech at a fraction of prior cost. That's a time-limited window to reclaim this infrastructure from the private sector and place it in the hands of the people using it.

What it captures

  • One-on-one conversations & commitment follow-through
  • Leadership development trajectories over time
  • Weekly organizer reports & coach responses
  • Team formation and dissolution
  • Event participation linked to individual contact records
  • Cross-organizational benchmarks (with partner consent)

How it ships

Staggered rollout across the POLIS network, alpha with 1–2 orgs, beta with 5–10, then full network, producing both the field-wide evidence base and the exogenous variation in adoption timing we use to identify effects. Infrastructure costs land around $50 – 200/month per organization, a fraction of incumbent alternatives.

Who's building it

A small forward-deployed engineering team, embedded with partner organizers, and shaped by prototype jams with collaborators including Cortico, Lumoviz, and Solidarity.tech. The platform is democratically governed, not investor-owned.

Program 02
Running · Cohort-based

Organizing workshops.

A structured training arc delivered across the POLIS network in collaboration with the Organizing Lab, building leadership density in real time while producing the data that lets us measure what training does.

Curriculum spine

  • Public narrative & the organizer's story of self
  • One-on-ones as a research method
  • Leadership teams and shared accountability
  • Strategy and the strategic chart
  • Measurement: what to count, and why

Phased delivery

Workshop timing is staggered across the network, letting us compare leadership-density trajectories across cohorts. Combined with Groundwork adoption, the 2 × 2 design (platform × training) lets us separately identify the effect of data infrastructure from the effect of training.

Built with

The Organizing Lab, with public-narrative and organizing-pedagogy lineage that has trained tens of thousands of organizers internationally.

Program 03
Emerging standard

Power metrics.

A small set of indicators that let organizations see their own power-building as clearly as they see their turnout, measured at the level of practice, not just output.

What we measure

  • Leadership density. The ratio of people taking durable leadership roles to the total membership engaged.
  • Commitment follow-through. The gap between what volunteers say yes to and what they do.
  • Relational network density. How connected the organization's base is to itself.
  • Coach-to-organizer ratio. A staff measure that predicts downstream leadership development.
  • Turf depth. The concentration of leaders in a given geography.

How orgs use it

Partner organizations receive quarterly benchmarks (anonymized across the network) and longitudinal tracking for their own practice. The goal is not a scoreboard, it's a language.

Program 04
Ongoing · Field-facing

Civic power & field collaborations.

The lab doesn't work alone. Our practice arm runs through a set of long-standing collaborations, with intermediaries, funders, and civic-tech peers, to build the field-owned infrastructure the moment demands.

Anchor collaborations

  • Democracy & Power Innovation Fund (DPI). Shared work on field-wide infrastructure, civic-tech governance, and funder practice. A primary partner on the Groundwork platform vision.
  • Organizing Lab. Workshop design and delivery; shared pedagogical spine; joint measurement of training effects.
  • Cortico · Lumoviz · Solidarity.tech. Peer civic-tech organizations with whom we convene open-source "prototype jams" to stress-test field tools.

Convenings

  • Prototype jams, Open working sessions with peer civic-tech orgs.
  • Learning circles, Cross-org reflection for POLIS partners.
  • Annual field meeting, Researchers, organizers, funders, together.
