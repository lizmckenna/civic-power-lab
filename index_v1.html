About the lab

Rigor in service of real movements.

We coined it, and we claim it: the R&D arm of democracy. A lab can't fill that whole space alone — but somebody has to insist it exists. We insist.

The work is mixed-methods by conviction, not habit: quantitative network and administrative data reveals the architecture of civic life, while deep ethnography reveals what it feels like to live inside it.

We publish in journals and we build tools organizers can actually pick up on a Tuesday. The lab runs on a simple premise: the study of democracy should be accountable to the people practicing it.

Housed at the Ash Center for Democratic Governance and Innovation at HKS, in long partnerships with community-based organizations that shape what we ask and how we ask it.
