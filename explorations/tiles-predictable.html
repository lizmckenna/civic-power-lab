Exploration · predictable tile patterns

Less random, more pattern.

Rotations follow a mirror rule — tile (c,r) rotation is decided by position, not by RNG. That's how Bulcão got the interlocking "lens" and "diamond" shapes across multiple tiles (like your second reference). Every tile in every band below is the same motif, at full density, with color as the only variable.

01 · quarter-disc

Lens pattern.

The mirror rule pairs adjacent quarter-discs into the lens shape you loved in the second reference. 100% density, mirror rotation.

1 color · teal
2 colors · teal + terracotta
3 colors · teal + terra + gold
02 · right triangle

Diamond pattern.

Same mirror rule applied to triangles — adjacent tiles mirror into big square-diagonal shapes. The most architectural variant (closest to your first reference).

1 color · teal
2 colors · teal + terracotta
3 colors · teal + terra + gold
03 · thick stripe

Woven pattern.

A centered bar per tile. The mirror rule turns it into a woven-looking grid — some bars vertical, some horizontal, following position. Evokes the bar tiles in your first reference.

1 color · teal
2 colors · teal + terracotta
3 colors · teal + terra + gold
Recommendation

The 2-color quarter-disc is the pick.

Teal + terracotta, mirror pattern, full density. It matches the second reference's interlocking lens figures exactly, gives the strip a predictable rhythm instead of noise, and the two-color treatment uses the whole Peacock + Terracotta palette without getting busy. Use the 1-color teal triangles for quiet section dividers. Save the 3-color variants for one hero moment, not every band.
