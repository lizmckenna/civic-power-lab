Exploration · tile streamline

Fewer tiles, more pattern.

The current Bulcão bands mix four to five motifs per strip with two or three colors at 0.85–0.92 density. Next to a dense logo, that reads as noise. Below are six alternatives that keep the modular-tile feel while turning the volume down. Each is shown in a strip and in a with-logo preview so you can see it next to the mark.

Arc only, one color.

The quarter-arc is the most archetypal Bulcão motif. Used alone, the random 90° rotations become the whole vocabulary — fullness emerges from orientation, not from variety.

Recommendation

Go with 01 · Arc only as the site-wide default.

Pair it with 02 (sparse arc) for quiet section dividers, and pull out 04 (arc + disc) only where the band needs more density (press-feature backgrounds, research project headers). Save multi-color combinations for one or two hero moments, not every strip.
